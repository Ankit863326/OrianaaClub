3. Membership Term and General Structure

3.1 25-Year Term

Each Membership is granted for a fixed period of twenty-five (25) years starting from the Effective Date specified in your Welcome Letter or Membership Certificate ("Membership Term").

For the avoidance of doubt:

  • No automatic renewal occurs at the end of the Membership Term.

  • Any extension, renewal or migration to a successor programme will be at Orianaa's discretion and on such terms (including further consideration) as may be offered at that time.

3.2 Activation and Cooling-Off Period

A Membership is treated as activated on the date recorded in the Welcome Letter, which will ordinarily follow receipt of the applicable joining fee (or first instalment, where an instalment plan applies) and completion of KYC. Orianaa will make reasonable efforts to issue the Welcome Letter and Membership Certificate within a reasonable period of activation.

Cooling-off period for online and offline sales. Whether the Membership Application Form is executed online, by telephone or through any other remote channel, or offline in person (including at an office, resort property, event or sales presentation), the Member may cancel the Membership within eight (8) days of the later of (a) the date of execution of the Membership Application Form, or (b) the date of receipt of the Welcome Letter, by written notice to Orianaa ("Cooling-Off Period"), without needing to show cause.

On a timely cancellation within the Cooling-Off Period, Orianaa will refund the joining fee actually paid, less reasonable processing charges disclosed at the time of sale and the value of any benefits already availed, within a reasonable period (not exceeding 30 days) of receipt of the cancellation notice.

After the Cooling-Off Period ends, cancellation is governed by Clause 14 (Suspension, Termination and Exit) and applicable consumer law.

3.3 Phasing of Benefits Over the Term

The Membership is structured in two broad phases:

  • Years 1–11: emphasis on high-impact, points-backed benefits, including use of a one-time bank of Joining Points to pay up to 50% of eligible bills, enhanced event benefits and promotional privileges; the flat 25% Members' Discount is available as an alternative on any bill;

  • Years 12–25: a sustained lifestyle layer with flat 25% member discounts and continued access and privileges, even after the Joining Points bank is exhausted or expired.

This two-phase structure is intended to balance early high-value usage with long-term affordability and continuity, similar to how long-horizon hospitality memberships often front-load promotional benefits but retain core privileges over time.

Discount and Points – one benefit per bill. The flat 25% Members' Discount is available throughout the 25-year Membership Term. Points redemption of up to 50% of an eligible bill is available only during Membership Years 1 to 11. The two cannot be used together: on any bill the Member may choose either the Members' Discount or Points redemption, but not both (see Clause 6.2).

Core Benefits vs Variable Benefits. For clarity across the 25-year Term, this Agreement distinguishes between:

  • "Core Benefits" — the flat 25% Members' Discount under Clause 4.2, the Tier-specific room-category entitlement and Maximum Stay Limit stated in the Tier Chart, and access to the Member Network — which remain guaranteed for the full Membership Term, subject only to AMC and dues being paid and this Agreement being complied with, and cannot be unilaterally withdrawn (though they may be adjusted under Clause 16.2 in a manner that preserves their core value); and

  • "Variable Benefits" — the Joining Points bank and its redemption terms (Clause 6), promotional privileges, specific partner tie-ups, Blackout Dates and seasonal calendars, and event or entertainment privileges — which may be revised, recalibrated or discontinued over the Term as reasonably necessary for commercial or operational reasons, with notice under Clause 16.2.

A Member may apply to upgrade to a higher Tier at any time during the Membership Term, subject to payment of the differential joining fee (if any) applicable at the time of upgrade, and subject to availability of the target Tier for fresh enrolment. Upon upgrade, unused Joining Points ordinarily carry forward, and any incremental points entitlement attributable to the higher Tier will be credited as notified at the time of upgrade.

Voluntary downgrades are not ordinarily available once a Tier has been activated, given the differentiated pricing and points structure across Tiers, save where Orianaa agrees to a downgrade in its discretion (for example, on grounds of genuine hardship), in which case the financial terms of the downgrade will be disclosed in writing before it takes effect.

4.5 Periodic Tier Review

Orianaa may periodically review Tier positioning and inventory allocation to reflect changes in property portfolio, demand patterns and operating costs. Any such review will be conducted consistently with Clause 4.2 (preservation of core Tier architecture) and Clause 16.2 (Amendments), and material adverse changes to a specific Member's Tier entitlements will be individually notified with reasonable prior notice.

4.6 Maximum Stay Limit and Points Utilisation

Each stay is subject to the maximum number of consecutive nights set for the Member's Tier in Clause 4.1 and the Tier Chart (the "Maximum Stay Limit"). The Maximum Stay Limit applies whether a stay is paid in cash, with Points, with the Members' Discount, or by any combination permitted under Clause 6.2.

A Member may use available Points in line with the applicable benefits and the redemption rules in Clause 6, but only within the Maximum Stay Limit. A larger Points balance does not increase the Maximum Stay Limit, and Points cannot be used to book nights beyond it.

Back-to-back or overlapping bookings by or for the same Member at the same property are treated as a single stay for this purpose. Any extension beyond the Maximum Stay Limit is at Orianaa's discretion, subject to availability, and on the terms Orianaa notifies at the time.

5. Fees, AMC and Financial Structure

5.1 Joining Fees and Plan Types

The one-time joining fee differs by Tier and plan type (for example, individual, core family, extended family, senior couple, corporate), as listed in the Points Schedule.

Founding tranches may enjoy enhanced Joining Points or promotional pricing; later cohorts may have different point banks or fee levels, as explicitly set out in the Points Schedule, which functions operationally like the "playing rights brochure" for club memberships where entry fees and subscription tiers are differentiated.

5.2 Instalment Facilities

Where Orianaa offers an instalment facility for payment of the joining fee, the specific schedule, any financing or processing charges, and the consequence of a missed instalment will be recorded in the Membership Application Form or a separate instalment schedule. Unless otherwise stated in that schedule, a Membership will not be treated as activated until the instalment amount required for activation (as disclosed at the time of sale) has been received. Once activated, and for so long as instalments remain outstanding, the Member's Points bank and booking access will be made available on a pro-rata basis corresponding to the proportion of the total joining fee actually received, rather than being withheld in full pending complete payment; on receipt of each subsequent instalment, the pro-rata Points and access will be adjusted upward accordingly. Where an instalment is missed, Clause 5.4 (Payment, Grace Periods and Consequences) applies by analogy to the missed instalment, and access is not automatically and entirely denied for that reason alone.

5.3 Annual Maintenance Charges (AMC)

AMC:

  • is waived for the first three (3) Membership Years across Tiers for Members enrolled during the launch/construction-stage of the relevant resort(s), reflecting the approved introductory policy for early members;

  • is payable from the Membership Year immediately following enrolment, for Members who enrol after the relevant resort(s) have become fully operational, in accordance with the final commercial policy notified to such Members at the time of enrolment [exact commencement trigger to be confirmed by management];

  • is charged on a category-wise basis (by Tier and plan type), and not on a per-person basis, as detailed in the Points Schedule;

  • is tier- and plan-specific, as detailed in the Points Schedule;

  • is subject to reasonable inflation-linked revision with notice, similar to revisions in professional body or club membership dues, where non-payment can lead to suspension of privileges or roll removal.

Holiday, wellness and other usage benefits are conditional on AMC and all other dues being fully paid at the time of booking and stay. Consumer fora have upheld an operator's right to deny holidays where members have not cleared AMC and joining-balance obligations, provided this linkage is clearly disclosed, as it is here.

5.4 Payment, Grace Periods and Consequences

The AMC due date each Membership Year is the anniversary of the Effective Date, or such other date as notified in the Welcome Letter or Points Schedule. Where AMC is not paid by its due date, the following phased process applies, running to a total of 365 days from the due date:

  • Grace Period (Days 1–60 from the due date): Orianaa will send payment reminders; Membership benefits continue uninterrupted and no restriction is applied.

  • Restriction / Suspension (Days 61–334 from the due date): if AMC remains unpaid after the Grace Period, booking privileges, points redemption and Member Network access are suspended (in accordance with Clause 14.1) until payment is received. The outstanding AMC continues to be owed, together with interest accruing under Clause 5.5, but no further Membership Year's AMC falls due, and no penalty beyond that interest accrues, while the Membership remains suspended on this ground.

  • Final Notice (by Day 335 from the due date): Orianaa will issue a final written notice specifying the outstanding amount (including accrued interest), the reactivation fee (if any) that would apply, and that the Membership will be terminated on Day 365 from the due date if the outstanding amount is not received.

  • Termination (Day 365 from the due date): if the outstanding AMC and accrued interest remain unpaid at Day 365 from the due date, Orianaa may terminate the Membership in accordance with Clause 14.2.

These phased consequences align with consumer-law expectations: operators are entitled to insist on AMC for service access, but abrupt one-sided forfeiture without notice or proportionality can be scrutinised and read down by consumer fora as unfair.

In summary: default on AMC leads only to a temporary restriction of discretionary benefits under Clause 14.1 (booking, points redemption and Member Network access) -- it does not accelerate future Membership Years' AMC and does not, by itself, affect the underlying 25-year Membership Term. Termination only follows the full phased process in this Clause 5.4 and is addressed in Clause 14.2, which does not result in automatic forfeiture of the joining fee. Interest on overdue AMC is dealt with in Clause 5.5, and the general refund position is set out in Clause 5.6.

5.5 Interest on Overdue Amounts

Where any amount payable by the Member (other than AMC governed by Clause 5.4) remains unpaid beyond its due date, Orianaa may levy reasonable interest on the overdue amount at a rate disclosed in the Points Schedule, calculated from the due date until payment, without prejudice to Orianaa's other rights under this Agreement.

5.6 Refunds

Save as expressly provided in this Agreement (including Clauses 3.2, 5.4, 5.8, 12.6, 12.7 and 14.2) or as required by applicable consumer law, joining fees and AMC already paid are non-refundable, reflecting that they compensate Orianaa for capacity reserved and services made available to the Member over the Membership Term. Termination for non-payment of AMC does not result in automatic or blanket forfeiture of the joining fee; the consequences are as set out in Clause 14.2. Where a consumer forum or court determines that a specific forfeiture is disproportionate on the facts, Orianaa will give effect to that determination.

5.7 Taxes and Invoicing

All fees and charges are exclusive of applicable GST and other statutory levies, which will be charged at prevailing rates.

Invoices may be raised by relevant Saptagiri Group Entities depending on the underlying service (for example, resort operations vs F&B vs wellness) but will be mapped to your central Membership ID for benefits and accounting.

6. Points System — Detailed Rules

6.1 Joining Points and Value

On activation, you receive a bank of Joining Points for your Tier and plan, as listed in the Points Schedule. Each point is notionally equivalent to one rupee of value when redeemed, subject to programme rules.

6.2 Points Usage Mechanics

Key mechanics include:

  • Maximum of 50% of the pre-tax eligible bill can be paid through Points per transaction, during Membership Years 1 to 11 only, subject to the no-stacking rule below;

  • No Stacking of Discounts and Points. Unless expressly stated otherwise for a specific offer, only one discount mechanism may be applied to a given transaction. Where a Member is eligible for the flat 25% Members' Discount under Clause 4.2 and also wishes to redeem Points against the same bill, the Member may apply either the discount or Points redemption, but not both, on the same bill or transaction. Promotional coupons, partner offers and time-limited campaigns cannot be combined with the Members' Discount or Points redemption unless the specific offer terms expressly say otherwise.

  • Points cannot be applied to:

    • taxes and statutory levies;

    • service charges or gratuities;

    • third-party vendor charges in events (for example décor vendors, outside caterers);

    • specifically designated fee items; redemption on Blackout Dates is permitted subject to availability and the applicable Points/value requirements, and is not prohibited merely because the date is a Blackout Date (see Clause 8.5).

  • Points can generally be redeemed against:

    • room rate components for eligible room types, within the Maximum Stay Limit (Clause 4.6);

    • vegetarian F&B and non-alcoholic beverages at in-house outlets;

    • wellness treatments and spa services at in-house centres;

    • curated cultural or entertainment experiences marked as points-eligible.

    • Events and Weddings hosted at Orianaa venues, and other approved products/services, subject to the exclusions in this Clause 6.2 (including third-party vendor charges).

6.3 Validity, Expiry and Devaluation

Joining Points are valid within an 11-year points window from activation, and can be redeemed only during Membership Years 1 to 11. Points not redeemed by the end of Membership Year 11 expire, subject to Clause 6.7 (Pre-Expiry Reminders for Points) and any extension under Clause 14.8 (Membership Pause).

To preserve commercial sustainability over a 25-year horizon, Orianaa may:

  • adjust the points-to-rupee conversion or category-wise redemption rates;

  • exclude or include certain services from points redemption; and

  • devalue points with reasonable prior notice via app/portal and registered channels, subject to the cap in this Clause 6.3.

Devaluation cap. Any single devaluation of the points-to-rupee conversion rate, or of category-wise redemption rates, will not exceed 10% in any rolling 12-month period, and Orianaa will give Members at least 60 days' prior notice before a devaluation takes effect, during which Members may redeem existing Points at the then-current (pre-devaluation) rate. This cap does not apply to the addition or removal of specific redemption categories under this Clause 6.3, which is addressed separately.

Such changes will be structured and communicated to avoid the kind of silent, opaque programme shifts that consumer fora and regulators have criticised as unfair trade practices in analogous loyalty and holiday membership contexts.

6.4 Points Statements and Reconciliation

Orianaa will make a running points statement available to each Member through the app or portal, showing accrual, redemption, expiry and balance. A Member who disputes a points entry must raise the dispute within a reasonable period (as notified in the app or portal) of the entry appearing, failing which the entry will be treated as accepted, without prejudice to any statutory right to raise the dispute later.

6.5 Points on Suspension or Termination

Where a Membership is suspended for non-payment under Clause 5.4, the associated points bank is also suspended for the duration of the suspension and reinstated on cure. Where a Membership is terminated under Clause 14, unredeemed points lapse with the Membership, without additional compensation, save where applicable consumer law requires otherwise on the specific facts of the termination.

6.6 Redemption Availability Commitment

Orianaa commits to maintaining reasonable redemption availability for Points across its resorts and eligible categories, having regard to overall inventory and demand. Without guaranteeing availability on any specific date, Orianaa will not operate the points-redemption system in a manner that renders a Member's Points illusory or incapable of practical use over the Membership Term taken as a whole, consistent with Clause 8.5 (Blackout Dates) and the devaluation protections in Clause 6.3.

6.7 Pre-Expiry Reminders for Points

For larger events, especially weddings and corporate gatherings, event-specific contracts will set out:

  • staged deposit milestones (for example, signing, mid-term and pre-event top-ups);

  • provisional and final headcount deadlines; and

  • treatment of deposits upon cancellation or material change, as set out in Clause 7.4.

These structures are designed so that charges retained upon late cancellation reasonably approximate the resort's lost opportunity and sunk preparation costs, aligning with jurisprudence that disfavors punitive "forfeit everything irrespective of circumstance" clauses.

7.4 Cancellation, Peak-Date Rules and No-Shows

Orianaa will maintain and publish a peak / super-peak / off-peak calendar by property and season, together with associated minimum-stay requirements and booking-window rules for room bookings, differentiated by rate plan and Member status as described in Clause 7.1.

Uniform 30-day cancellation slab for weddings and events. For all wedding, banquet and event bookings under Clause 7.3, irrespective of venue category or season, the following single cancellation slab applies, calculated by reference to the number of days between the cancellation notice and the scheduled event date:

  • 30 days or more before the event: the Member is entitled to a refund or, at the Member's election, an equivalent credit against a future booking, of all amounts paid less any deposit already applied to non-recoverable third-party commitments (for example, vendor advances placed on the Member's specific instructions under Clause 7.7), which will be itemised and evidenced on request;

  • less than 30 days before the event: amounts paid are retained by Orianaa to the extent they reasonably approximate Orianaa's sunk preparation costs and lost opportunity for that date, save that Orianaa will use reasonable efforts to re-let the date and refund or credit any amount it is able to recover by doing so, net of reasonable re-letting costs.

This single 30-day threshold replaces any differentiated peak/super-peak/off-peak retention slabs for event cancellations, so that Members face one clear, predictable rule regardless of season or venue category. No-shows (a booking neither used nor cancelled) are treated as a cancellation received on the event date itself, i.e. within the less-than-30-day band.

These rules aim to avoid systemic conduct where membership operators repeatedly cite "peak period" to deny bookings or refunds without offering realistic alternatives, a pattern that consumer fora have condemned as deficiency in service and unfair trade practice.

7.5 Booking Modifications

Requests to change dates, upgrade room category, increase headcount, or make similar modifications after confirmation will be accommodated where reasonably possible, subject to availability and the applicable policy. A modification request does not, by itself, result in automatic cancellation of the original booking or deduction of cancellation charges. The cancellation terms under Clause 7.4 will apply to a modification, and then only to the specific portion being changed (not as an arbitrary partial deduction), where the modification genuinely amounts to a fresh booking on materially different terms (for example, a substantially later date wholly outside the original booking window and season); this will be disclosed to the Member, with reasons, before the modification is confirmed.

7.6 Overbooking and Alternative Accommodation

In the rare event that a confirmed booking cannot be honoured due to genuine operational constraints (for example, unforeseen property closure), Orianaa will offer, at the Member's option, either a comparable alternative property or room category for the same dates at no additional cost, or rescheduling to alternative dates with any applicable rate protected, or a refund of amounts paid for that specific booking together with reasonable compensation for demonstrated additional cost directly caused by the disruption, consistent with consumer-law expectations regarding deficiency in service.

  • Effect on Membership and Points: during a pause, (i) no AMC falls due for the paused period, and the AMC due date is deferred by the length of the pause on resumption; (ii) the Points validity window under Clause 6.3 is extended by the length of the pause; (iii) the Membership Term under Clause 3.1 is extended by the length of the pause; and (iv) booking privileges, Points redemption, the Members' Discount, Guest Cards and Member Network access are suspended for the duration of the pause; and (v) annual allowances (such as naturopathy visits, guest cards and peak-season bookings) for the Membership Year in which the pause falls are reduced pro rata, and allowances for the paused period do not carry forward;

  • Conditions: a pause does not affect accrued Points balances, which remain available on resumption, and does not entitle the Member to any refund of AMC or joining fee already paid for the period preceding the pause.

  • Confirmed bookings: bookings falling within the pause period must be cancelled before the pause starts, on the applicable cancellation terms under Clause 7;

  • Resumption: the Membership resumes automatically at the end of the approved pause. The Member may resume early on fifteen (15) days' written notice, once the pause has lasted at least three (3) months.
