id="s21">4.4 Tier Upgrades and Downgrades</h3>
<p>A Member may apply to upgrade to a higher Tier at any time during the Membership Term, subject to payment of the differential joining fee (if any) applicable at the time of upgrade, and subject to availability of the target Tier for fresh enrolment. Upon upgrade, unused Joining Points ordinarily carry forward, and any incremental points entitlement attributable to the higher Tier will be credited as notified at the time of upgrade.</p>
